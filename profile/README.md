# ReachOptimum@TEMA

**ReachOptimum** is a multidisciplinary research group at the **Centre for Mechanical Technology and Automation (TEMA), University of Aveiro**. We explore optimization, data-driven decision support, and intelligent systems applied to real industrial and water-infrastructure challenges.

---

## 🚀 What we do
- 🎯 **Optimization & Operations Research** – metaheuristics, multi-objective optimization, robust/sustainable decision-making  
- 🤖 **AI/ML for Engineering** – predictive modeling, explainable AI, digital twins  
- 💧 **Water Systems** – efficiency, control, and resilience in WSS and distribution networks  
- 🔓 **Open Science & Reproducibility** – open datasets, notebooks, and reference implementations
